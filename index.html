Privacy and data

Saved on this device only: chats, notes, tasks, theme and custom instructions. Internet-first lookups send the key words of your question straight from your browser to public services (Wikipedia, Open-Meteo weather, ExchangeRate-API, dictionaryapi.dev). Only when AI is needed is your message sent to Puter and the AI provider (this includes web search for news and prices). Voice input uses your browser's speech service, which may process audio online. No passwords or API keys are stored here.
